## 1. Decisions the team must make in this meeting

| # | Decision | Constraint from the project doc |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Scrum Master** | One person. Their dev workload is 30% less than others — but they still must commit code. They own: story-point estimation facilitation, velocity + burndown charts, sprint forecast tables to the PO, attendance sheets, the Member Participation Report, GitHub setup + ownership transfer, and *all* Canvas submissions. Changing SM mid-project is allowed but discouraged. |
| 2 | **Top 5 project bids** (EoI, Friday) | ~14 projects, ~11 run. **Roughly half of teams get a project they did not bid for.** Bid for breadth of skill fit, not passion for #1. |
| 3 | **Team name + portfolio content** | Group photo, member list + contacts, SM named, one paragraph + portrait per member (study, work experience, technical skills). |
| 4 | **Weekly working rhythm** | Two fixed full-ish days/week, ~10 h per person per week inclusive of everything; ~6–8 h of that is actual dev. Labs Tue 10–12 and Wed 10–12 are the assumed meeting window. |
| 5 | **Sprint start weekday** | Sprints are **fixed one week**. Demos run Wed in lecture time → start sprints Thursday, end Wednesday. This must be stated in the Project Proposal. |
| 6 | **E-board tool** (Jira or Trello) | Mandatory digital board, must be live and current at all times, PO + teaching team get access. |
| 7 | **Comms channels** | One for team chat, one for PO. Plus a shared drive for evidence. |

## 3. Every sprint demo (Sprints 3–6) must contain — build the deck template once
1. Sprint plan of the sprint that just finished
2. Demo of stories marked **Done** per the Definition of Done in the proposal
3. **Velocity history** bar chart — planned vs actual, per sprint
4. **Sprint burndown chart**
5. Explanation of any significant velocity change
6. Per-member attendance and participation from the SM's Member Participation Report

5 minutes, 2–3 presenters, rotating. **Every member must present at least once across Sprints 3–6, or they get zero for sprint demos.**

## 4. Standing evidence log — start it in Week 2, not Week 10
Assessment is holistic and evidence-based. The SM must be able to produce, on request:
- attendance sheet for every meeting and standup, per sprint
- hours per member per sprint (each member self-reports weekly — set a Friday reminder)
- sprint forecast tables sent to the PO after each planning meeting
- velocity + burndown history
- e-board task allocation history

Final presentation requires proving **team size × 10 × 7 = 560 h** total and **70 h per member**, consistent across items 9, 10, 11 of the final presentation. That number cannot be reconstructed retroactively without it looking fabricated.
