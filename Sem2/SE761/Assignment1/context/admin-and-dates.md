## Gen AI policy

- **Permitted and encouraged.** "Using ChatGPT to improve your productivity is a valuable skill in itself."
- **2 marks** for elaborating how it was used - prompts, process, where it helped and where it did not.
- "No matter how you used it, you won't be penalised."
- ⚠ **The reverse of A2**, which discourages it for content.
- Turnitin is used, including peer-to-peer and past/future cohort comparison.
- The brief's own joke: "Not your friend's work (unless ChatGPT is your only friend)."
