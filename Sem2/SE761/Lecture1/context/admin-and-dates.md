# SOFTENG 761 Lecture 1 - Admin and dates

Quarantined noise. This deck is roughly 75% administration; all of it is here and none of it is quizzable.

## Course structure and weighting

- **Modules 1 & 2** (Project Rationale, Understanding Agile) - Paramvir Singh. Weeks 1-3. Three written assessments. **20%** of the course.
- **Module 3** (Project) - Reza Shahamiri. Weeks 4-11. Practical team project. **80%** of the course.
- No final exam. Framed as "80% project, 20% lecture".

## Project deliverables

| Deliverable | Marks |
|---|---|
| Team Portfolios | 0 on its own - folded into Project Proposal |
| Project Proposal (includes portfolios) | 15 |
| Sprint Demos (4 x 2) | 8 |
| Final Demo and Presentation (10 of these allocated individually) | 12 |
| Code Quality and Professionalism | 15 |
| Individual Reflection | 5 |
| Participation Report and Peer-Review | 25 |
| Scrum Master's Member Participation Report | weekly, feeds assessment |

- **Holistic assessment.** Everything is connected: "if you have the perfect project proposal but you deliver a very crappy project, you will end up with a low holistic score." Code quality does not just cost its own 15 marks - it reduces the holistic score across the whole project.
- **Individual differentiation.** Three evidence streams: GitHub contribution to the default branch, the Scrum Master's weekly participation report, and confidential peer reviews (7 per student in a team of 8). Students who did not code receive no mark "no matter what else they did". Above-and-beyond contribution can earn bonus marks. Team members have failed while teammates got A+.
- **Attendance is mandatory** for the Sprint 7 final demo; unexcused absence carries a penalty.

## Teams and projects

- **Teams of eight.** Formed on Canvas by end of week 1 - paper teams do not count. Unallocated students randomly assigned. Teams essentially cannot be changed afterwards.
- ~14 projects available, ~11 expected to run.
- Bid for your **top five preferences** via EoI, due end of week 2 (Friday). Roughly **half of teams receive a project they did not bid for** - so build for a diverse skill set, not for your first preference.
- Some projects are new builds, some are maintenance/continuation projects.
- Appoint a **Scrum Master** in week 1.
- Team Portfolios due week 2 (Sunday); submitted again as part of the Project Proposal in week 3.
- Project allocation finalised early week 3; Project Agreement form signed and submitted on Canvas.
- Product Owners have each been asked to allocate **one hour per week** to meet the team.
