## Deadlines and weightings

- **Individual Goal Setting assignment — due Monday 27 July 2026, 10:00 pm.** Stated twice (opening and closing slides).

## Course logistics

- Lectures: Tuesday 1–2 pm · Thursday 2–3 pm · Friday 1–2 pm.
- Course arc by week: Systems Thinking → Solution Analysis → Project Execution → Systems Week.
  - Week 1: mindset, grit, systems thinking
  - Weeks 1–3: strategic case, economic case, financial case, government context
  - Week 4: management case, technical know-how
  - Week 5: team building
  - Week 6: Team Project (the "dress rehearsal")
  - Week 7: Systems Week, plus a McKinsey workshop
- Two week-long projects: Team Project (warm-up) and Systems Project.
- Systems Week: teams of ~30, one week, one large problem. Team Project is the deliberate practice run beforehand.
- Team formation deadline was flagged as imminent; incomplete teams to be randomly assigned.
- **The clinic:** all three teaching staff available 9–5 for questions — content problems, team breakdowns, absent members, replacing a sick team leader.
- Cohort size ~800.
- Contact via the Contact Form.
