## Gen AI policy - the two-lane model

- **Lane 1 (controlled assessment): AI is restricted.** The only Lane 1 assessment in this course is **lecture participation** - i.e. actually being present at the two in-person lectures.
- **Lane 2 (uncontrolled assessment): AI may be used to assist student learning.** Everything else.
- For the **Team Project and Systems Week**: the **team must agree** how to use AI, and must complete a **Reflection of AI Usage**, attached to the front matter of submitted reports.
- Faculty-wide policy instituted this past year.

### The lecturer's Gen AI benchmark

Both the team project and the systems project are given to a couple of Gen AI systems, which read, solve, peer-review each other's output and produce the best possible result **with no human input**. That output is then independently marked.

**Last year it scored a D+.** The test is repeated annually. Stated conclusion: "if you're thinking that you can get AI to do these assessments - not yet."

### Endorsed uses of Gen AI

Brainstorming and idea generation in early stages; sense-checking; help with tone and framing; abbreviating overlong text; image generation. The slide's own co-pilot-generated images are credited as role-modelling. Conditions: the team has agreed, everyone knows who is doing what, and usage is clearly stated. "It's not a cheat. It's not a don't use it. It's a **use it smartly**."

## Academic integrity
