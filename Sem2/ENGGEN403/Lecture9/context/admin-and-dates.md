## In-lecture activity

- The class built a causal loop for **high food prices** on printed sheets divided into four quadrants: **production, transport, retail/consumers, government**.
- Three timed blocks of five minutes: brainstorm 8-10 variables → draw arrows and signs → label one reinforcing or balancing loop.
- Three teams fed back a loop each, using a soft-cube microphone passed around the room.
- **After-class activity (slide 23):** complete the causal loop diagram, label at least one R or B loop, identify one or two leverage points, and use those insights to develop a systems problem statement.
