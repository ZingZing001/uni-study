# 6. Executive summary — ⭐ 30% of the mark
**Feeds:** report §1 · **Owner:** *TBC — name someone on Monday*

> **300–500 words. One page maximum. Worth as much as the entire Strategic Case.**

---
## The three rubric criteria
| Criterion | Exemplary |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | Summarises **all essential information** from the report |
| **Recommendations** | **Clear recommendation of the shortlisted options** to investigate further |
| ⭐ **Stand alone** | Reader can **make a decision without reading the body** |

## Must contain
- [ ] **Purpose** — who asked, what for
- [ ] **Methods** — how we got there, briefly
- [ ] **Recommendations** — the shortlist
- [ ] **Costs** — order of magnitude
- [ ] Items in **the same order they appear in the report**
- [ ] ⭐ **Ends with the proposed shortlist and "what next"**

## Process
1. **Draft early** as a statement of intent — it forces the team to agree what we're actually saying
2. **Rewrite at the end** against what the report actually says
3. ⭐ **Test:** hand it to someone who did not write it. Ask them to state our recommendation and why. If they can't, it fails the criterion carrying 30%
